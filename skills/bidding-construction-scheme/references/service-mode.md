# Service-scheme mode

Use this mode when the task is chiefly a continuing service rather than physical construction. Do not force a construction-template critical path simply because a BOQ exists.

## Classification signals

Service signals include staffing/attendance, inspection, operation, maintenance response, cleaning, transport, consulting, testing-as-a-service, records, SLA/KPI, recurring cycles, and service acceptance. A mixed project may still have installation or repair packages; separate them explicitly.

## Service technical-bid structure

Use the latest body typography/page target unless the service tender specifies otherwise, but do not force 15 construction chapters and six construction appendices onto a service assignment. Build a service requirement map and equivalent read-only format/page audit. The construction-only `audit_latest_format.py` cannot certify service-topic completeness; report that boundary rather than waive construction checks silently.

Adapt the template while preserving its formatting contract. Cover:

- understanding of objectives, scope, sites, interfaces, exclusions, and service period;
- service organization, positions, competencies, shifts, mobilization, backup staffing, and communication routes;
- service process from request/intake through dispatch, execution, verification, closure, records, and review;
- recurring inspection/maintenance cycles and priority/severity classification;
- response times, escalation, continuity, KPI/SLA measurement, and evidence retention;
- quality assurance, customer communication, complaints, corrective action, and continual improvement;
- HSE controls appropriate to the service locations and activities;
- materials/tools/vehicles/IT systems and owner-provided interfaces;
- emergency and business-continuity arrangements;
- transition-in, steady operation, transition-out, handover, and confidentiality/data controls.

## Service workbook

Retain the five required worksheet names when the user asks for the established paired deliverable, but reinterpret them honestly:

- `横道进度计划`: mobilization, recurring cycles, reviews, deliverables, and transition milestones;
- `网络计划逻辑`: request-to-close workflow and phase dependencies;
- `资源计划`: staffing/shift/vehicle/tool coverage;
- `评标响应索引`: tender criteria traceability;
- `清单工程量摘要`: service items, frequency, unit, estimated quantity/workload, and response reference.

Do not invent a construction sequence, temporary works, or commissioning activities that do not apply.
