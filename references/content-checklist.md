# Construction Scheme Content Checklist

## Minimum Chapters

### 一、编制依据及工程概况

Include project name, location/area, contractor, dates, inclusive duration, construction objectives, source materials, applicable standards, BOQ-derived scope, key quantities, site/production constraints, interfaces, assumptions, and confirmation items.

### 二、施工组织体系

Include organization chart or role table, project manager responsibilities, technical/quality/safety/material responsibilities, reporting route, shifts, permit coordination, and coordination with owner/production/maintenance. Preserve supplied personnel names.

### 三、施工预设目标及组织管理体系

State safety, quality, duration, environmental, civilized-construction, and document-control objectives. Explain planning, daily coordination, material traceability, hold points, and change control.

### 四、主要施工工序及方法

Write the longest chapter. Use only applicable subsections:

- survey, technical disclosure, permits, temporary works, and protection;
- shutdown, LOTO, medium evacuation, cleaning, neutralization, gas detection, and handover;
- demolition sequence, segmentation, temporary support, lifting, transport, and waste classification;
- earthwork, concrete, reinforcement, formwork, embedded parts, grout, and restoration;
- structural steel fabrication, installation, welding, bolting, coating, platforms, ladders, and rails;
- equipment unpacking, lifting, alignment, leveling, grouting, connection, and standalone tests;
- metallic and nonmetallic piping, supports, fusion/welding, valves, compensators, tests, flushing, and cleanliness;
- cable trays, conduit, panels, cable pulling, termination, grounding, fire sealing, and insulation tests;
- instrumentation, impulse tubing, loops, PLC/interlocks, calibration, and functional testing;
- anticorrosion substrate preparation, environmental conditions, layer sequence, curing, holiday/appearance/thickness checks;
- water features, landscaping, drainage, roads, buildings, or other project-specific disciplines;
- integrated commissioning, production switchover, restoration, as-built records, and handover.

For every major method, explain preconditions, tools/resources, sequence, technical parameters, quality checks, safety controls, records, and acceptance.

### 五、技术质量标准

Define applicable acceptance standards, material certificates, inspection lots, hold/witness points, allowable deviations where verified, tests, records, defect handling, and final acceptance package. Embed selected standard-PDF excerpts with interpretation.

### 六、质量保证措施

Explain sample approval, incoming inspection, traceability, process inspection, stop-point release, measurement calibration, nonconformance control, finished-product protection, document control, and punch-list closure.

### 七、安全环保措施

Include hazard identification and risk controls. Cover applicable work at height, lifting, hot work, confined space, temporary electricity, cross operations, chemicals/acids, pressure testing, demolition, moving machinery, fire, falling objects, environmental pollution, noise/dust, waste, and production interfaces.

Use a hazard matrix with at least: activity, hazard, consequence, risk level, controls, and responsible role. Add narrative for major risks instead of relying only on the table.

### 八、资源需求计划

List manpower by trade/phase, main machines, lifting equipment, test instruments, temporary facilities, PPE, emergency materials, and material arrival sequence. Tie resources to the schedule and BOQ.

### 九、应急预案

Include command structure, communication, alarm/reporting, site isolation, shutdown/energy isolation, evacuation and muster, first aid, external rescue, and recovery. Add scenario procedures for applicable acid leakage/exposure, fire, electric shock, fall, lifting injury, confined-space incident, mechanical injury, pressure release, and environmental spill.

Do not invent personal phone numbers. Use roles or placeholders unless verified contacts are supplied.

### 十、施工进度计划

State the inclusive duration, milestones, critical constraints, delay controls, weekly/daily rolling plan, material/procurement interfaces, and a readable Gantt schedule through completion and handover.

## Professional Depth Tests

- A reviewer should be able to understand how work starts, proceeds, is checked, and is handed over.
- BOQ quantities should inform the methods and resources rather than merely appearing in a summary table.
- The scheme should distinguish contract quantity, drawing-controlled quantity, site verification, and manufacturer requirements.
- High-risk operations must have method, permit, isolation, monitoring, stop-work, and emergency controls.
- Tables organize information; prose explains engineering judgment.
