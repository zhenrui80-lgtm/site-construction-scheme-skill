# Construction Scheme Content Checklist

## Complete Content Framework

Cover all fifteen modules. The selected template may change numbering and visual hierarchy, but it may not delete a module.

### 1. 编制依据、工程概况与范围边界

Include project name, location/area, contractor, dates, inclusive duration, objectives, source materials, current standards, BOQ-derived scope and key quantities, production constraints, interfaces, assumptions, exclusions, and drawing/site/manufacturer confirmation items.

### 2. 施工部署与组织机构

Include an organization chart or one compact role table only when it improves lookup. Explain responsibilities, reporting route, shifts, permit coordination, stop-work authority, owner/production/maintenance interfaces, and preserved supplied personnel names in prose.

### 3. 施工准备、总平面与临时设施

Explain survey and review, technical disclosure, permits, access, material storage, fabrication area, lifting routes, temporary electricity/water/lighting, fire protection, barriers, signage, drainage, waste areas, and restoration. Include a useful layout or route schematic when site information permits.

### 4. 进度计划与工期保证

State milestones, critical path, shutdown windows, procurement and fabrication lead times, weekly/daily rolling control, delay recovery, interface handoffs, and a landscape Gantt through testing, restoration, and handover.

### 5. 主要施工工序与专项工法

This is the longest chapter. Create one numbered subsection for every major BOQ work package. Use only applicable disciplines, including demolition, civil work, structure, equipment, lifting, piping, nonmetallic piping, electrical, instrumentation, anticorrosion, building/decoration, drainage, landscaping, water feature, testing, and production switchover.

For every major method, include all eight elements:

1. preconditions and handover state;
2. people, machines, tools, materials, and temporary works;
3. detailed sequence and cross-discipline interfaces;
4. verified technical parameters and drawing/manufacturer-controlled values;
5. quality checks, hold/witness points, and defect handling;
6. safety, monitoring, isolation, and stop-work conditions;
7. records, reports, traceability, and sign-off;
8. acceptance and handover criteria.

For shutdown or hazardous-media work, also cover LOTO, evacuation/draining, cleaning/neutralization, detection, blind plates or physical isolation, handover certificates, and restoration. For demolition, cover segmentation, temporary support, weight/control assumptions, lifting/transport, and classified disposal.

### 6. 技术标准、质量管理与创优

Define acceptance standards, incoming inspection, certificates, samples, inspection lots, hold/witness points, allowable deviations where verified, calibration, tests, nonconformance control, punch-list closure, and the final quality dossier. Every inserted standard excerpt needs project-specific interpretation.

### 7. 安全管理、危险源辨识与专项控制

Include safety responsibility, permits, daily disclosure, inspection, stop-work, and one consolidated hazard matrix with activity, hazard, consequence, risk level, controls, and responsible role. Do not split risks into many small tables. Add narrative controls for applicable work at height, lifting, hot work, confined space, temporary electricity, cross operations, hazardous media, pressure tests, demolition, moving machinery, falling objects, fire, and production interfaces.

### 8. 文明施工、环境与职业健康

Explain enclosure and access, housekeeping, dust/noise/wastewater controls, waste segregation, spill prevention, hazardous waste, occupational exposure, PPE, heat/cold stress, lighting, sanitation, monitoring, complaints, and restoration.

### 9. 劳动力、材料、机械与检测资源

Tie manpower by trade and phase, main machines, lifting equipment, test instruments, temporary facilities, PPE, emergency materials, material submittals, fabrication/procurement, and arrival sequence to the BOQ and schedule. Use at most one manpower table and one combined equipment/instrument table unless the scope clearly requires separation. Explain mobilization and allocation logic in prose. Do not invent named personnel.

### 10. 季节性及特殊条件施工

Cover only applicable rain, heat, winter, strong wind, lightning, poor lighting, indoor production, corrosive atmosphere, restricted space, and live-plant conditions. State monitoring thresholds only when verified.

### 11. 成品、既有设施与生产系统保护

Explain protection boundaries, temporary covers/supports, fire blankets, contamination control, cable/instrument protection, access control, transfer of custody, damage reporting, restoration, and final joint inspection.

### 12. 应急预案

Include command structure, communication, alarm/reporting, isolation, shutdown/energy isolation, evacuation and muster, first aid, external rescue, environmental containment, recovery, drills, and supplies. Add project-specific scenario procedures. Do not invent phone numbers.

### 13. 交叉施工及各方协调

Define owner, production, supervision, design, manufacturer, subcontractor, inspection, and adjacent-contractor interfaces; permit and drawing release; shutdown confirmation; daily coordination; change control; shared lifting/access areas; and dispute/escalation routes.

### 14. 试验、调试、竣工验收与资料移交

Explain inspection release, pressure/leak/insulation/grounding/loop tests as applicable, flushing and cleanliness, standalone and integrated commissioning, interlocks, no-load/load tests, restoration, as-built drawings, records, training, punch-list closure, and handover.

### 15. 保修服务与履约承诺

State defect response, warranty boundaries, spare/consumable handover, follow-up inspection, production support, document retention, and closure responsibilities without inventing contractual periods.

## Project-Specific Drafting Contract

- Map every major BOQ item to method, quality, safety, resources, schedule, and acceptance content.
- Use 6-12 project-specific terms and 8-16 method terms as drafting anchors and final audit inputs.
- Distinguish contract quantity, drawing-controlled quantity, site verification, and manufacturer requirements.
- Explain engineering judgment in prose; use tables only for genuinely comparative or repeated data and pass the table-admission test in `table-policy.md`.
- Do not paste the same generic paragraph under different headings.
- A reviewer must be able to understand how each major work package starts, proceeds, is checked, is stopped when unsafe, and is handed over.
